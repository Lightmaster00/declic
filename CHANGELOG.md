# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release of Declic, a free and open-source screenshot app for macOS 14+.

- Area, window and full-screen capture, with a magnifier and a window picker.
- Scrolling capture that follows the pane under the pointer, scrolling down or up.
- Video (MP4) and GIF recording of an area, the screen or an app.
- Quick Access thumbnails with copy, save, keep and drag and drop.
- Annotation editor with zoom.
- Capture history.
- Automatic updates from GitHub releases.
- Interface in English, French, Spanish, German and Japanese.
