Everything you need, nothing more.

One shortcut, and your capture is ready to paste, annotate or send.

Area, window, screen

Draw a pixel-perfect area with the magnifier, or pick any open window.

Scrolling capture

A whole conversation, web page or email: just scroll, Déclic stitches it into one image.

Video and GIF

Record an area, the screen or an app, with the cursor and clicks. Export as MP4 or GIF.

Annotations

Arrows, shapes, text, highlighter, blur, numbered steps and crop. Pinch to zoom into the details.

Quick Access

Every capture waits in a corner: copy it, save it, drag it into any app, or keep it around.

History

Find all your recent captures and videos, even the ones you didn't save.

All from the keyboard.

Every shortcut can be changed in Settings.
