# Déclic — brand guide

## Name
**Déclic** is French for the *click* of a camera shutter — and for the moment something suddenly
makes sense. Written "Déclic" with the accent; in technical places (files, identifiers): `declic`.

## Promise
**Capture, annotate, share — in one click.**

Three pillars to repeat everywhere:
1. **Fast** — one shortcut, a capture ready to paste.
2. **Complete** — area, window, scrolling, video, GIF, annotations.
3. **Free** — open source (MIT), no account, nothing leaves your Mac.

## Voice
Direct, warm, informal. Short sentences. No jargon and no empty superlatives ("revolutionary",
"ultimate"). Show rather than claim.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coral | `#FF5B3A` | primary color, call-to-action buttons, links |
| Apricot | `#FF8A3D` | gradient start |
| Raspberry | `#E8344E` | gradient end, accents |
| Ink | `#16151D` | text, dark backgrounds |
| Paper | `#FBF7F2` | light backgrounds |

Signature gradient: apricot → coral → raspberry, diagonal (top left → bottom right).

## Symbol
A viewfinder: four rounded corners with nothing in the middle — what matters is what you frame.
- App icon: white symbol on the gradient, macOS shape (`Branding/icon.svg`).
- Menu bar: the same symbol, monochrome (drawn in `Brand.swift`).
- Clear space: at least the length of one viewfinder corner around the symbol.

## Typography
SF Pro (the system font) in the app and on the website; headings in *Bold*, *Rounded* for the
"Déclic" name.
